# Spotineil privacy policy

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

Spotineil is a student project: an Android music app that plays music from free, legal sources.

## What the app collects
**Nothing about you is sent to the developer.** Spotineil has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

## What stays on your phone
These are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data:
- your Liked Songs, followed podcasts, favourite radio stations and play history;
- where you stopped in a YouTube song ("Continue listening");
- temporary copies of song lists (cache) so the app loads faster and uses less data;
- your settings (theme, language).

## Services the app talks to
To show and play music, the app sends requests (such as your search words) directly to the services below. Each one handles those requests under its own privacy policy.
- **YouTube** (YouTube API Services and YouTube's embedded player). Spotineil uses YouTube API Services. By using YouTube features you agree to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies.
- **Jamendo, Audius, Internet Archive, ccMixter**: free and Creative Commons music.
- **Deezer, iTunes, Last.fm**: charts and 30-second previews.
- **Radio Browser**: radio station directory.
- **Apple Podcasts directory** and each podcast's own RSS feed.
- **LRCLIB**: song lyrics.

Spotineil does not access your YouTube, Google or other accounts. If you choose to log in with Spotify (optional, from Settings), the login happens on Spotify's own page, and Spotineil never sees your password.

## Revoking access
Spotineil never accesses your Google account. You can still review third-party access at <https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions>. To delete everything Spotineil stored, clear the app's data in Android Settings or uninstall it.
